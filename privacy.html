Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy describes how Sharaido Games ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile application Spiral Flow (the "App").

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Spiral Flow is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers beyond what the platform (Apple) provides for analytics
  • Photos, contacts, or other personal files

2. Analytics & Crash Reporting

The App may use Apple's built-in analytics and crash reporting tools (subject to your device-level privacy settings). This data is anonymized and aggregated; it cannot be used to identify you personally. We use it solely to improve App stability and performance.

If you have opted out of sharing analytics with developers on your Apple device, no analytics data is collected.

3. Advertising

If the App includes advertisements, they are served through third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob). These networks may collect certain information as described in their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies. We do not share any personal data with advertisers.

4. In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases are processed directly by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs the purchase process.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to retain or delete. Any anonymized analytics data is retained by Apple per their own data policies.

7. Third-Party Services

The App may link to or integrate with third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services and encourage you to read their policies independently.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
