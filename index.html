The Dark Crown: Genesis is a 2D pixel-art action rogue-lite set in a dying kingdom swallowed by darkness. Each run, you go deeper, hit harder, and forge something new from the ruins.

No two descents play the same. Weapons, relics, and blessings combine in ways that reward experimentation and punish hesitation. Die. Learn. Reign.
